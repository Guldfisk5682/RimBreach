# RimBreach

RimBreach is a RimWorld mod built on IsoRimZ and Combat Extended. It adds artillery-supported raids, multi-phase encirclement sieges, and fortress assault quests for **RimWorld 1.7 Unstable**.

## Artillery-supported raids

Human and mechanoid raiders can establish artillery positions to support their main assault. Stronger raids can bring heavier guns, and eligible raiders can carry folding ladders to scale your walls.

## Encirclement sieges

Late-game hostile factions can launch multi-phase encirclements that blockade your airspace, establish fortified rear camps with harassment devices and barracks, push forward heavy artillery batteries under smoke or shield cover, and assemble waves for a final assault.

## Fortress assaults

Take on a contract to capture a fortified outpost for a reward, or destroy a hostile fortress that sends raiders against your colony. Fight through layered defenses with manned guns, towers, and a central keep. The garrison can reinforce threatened positions, fall back to inner defenses, and repair damage during lulls.

## Requirements

- RimWorld Biotech
- [Harmony](https://github.com/pardeike/HarmonyRimWorld)
- [Combat Extended](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtended)
- [Combat Extended Armory](https://github.com/CombatExtended-Continued/CombatExtendedArmory)
- [IsoRim Waylith](https://github.com/peren717/IsoRim-1.7), required by IsoRimZ
- [IsoRimZ](https://github.com/peren717/IsoRim-ProjectZ)
- [IsoRim Waylith - Combat Extended Pack](https://github.com/peren717/IsoRimWaylith-CEPack), with extension API 1

Load RimBreach after these mods.
